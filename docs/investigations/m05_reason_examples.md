## What changes, in short

- **Size stops crowding out the reasons.** Against all banks, any large bank's size looked unusual (e.g. "$15.3bn vs $146m"); against its own tier it rarely does, so asset quality and earnings come through.
- **Odd values are less likely to lead.** E.g. the old method's "Loans to deposits 3,525,900%" for Bank of America California (a ratio broken by near-zero deposits).
- **Mostly the same picture:** the top reason is the same feature for 21 of the 30 banks; the order and the third reason change most. Size is cited 8 times by the old method, 3 times by the new.
